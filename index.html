01 / WHO WE ARE
THE JMK APPROACH

Built for complex
questions.

JMK Consulting Limited is a research and consulting firm providing specialized services to the business community, NGOs, MMDAs and other organizations in Ghana and abroad.

JMK combines local knowledge and expertise with rigorous research, monitoring and evaluation, data systems, organizational development and business development support. Its stated objectives include offering objective, innovative and quality research services, full-service market research, baseline research, basic research and support for NGO and small-business programmes.

What JMK promises
  • Strong motivation and dedication to deliver exceptional outcomes.
  • Value for money and high returns on consulting investment.
  • A partnership approach that designs and embeds lasting solutions.
  • A pragmatic focus on tangible benefits.
